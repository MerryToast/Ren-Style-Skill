# Translation Guide (Chinese → English)

1. **Restructure, don't transliterate.** Chinese topic-comment order → English subject-verb order;
   split long Chinese run-on sentences at logical boundaries (target the section's sentence-length range
   in `style_profile.json`).
2. **Section-specific tense** — follow `style_profile.md` (to be filled from stats).
3. **Findings** — render "结果表明 / 研究发现" with Ren's formal-subject frames ("It is shown that …").
4. **Apparatus** — "自行设计的试验装置" → "our newly designed test rig".
5. **Articles & plurals** — add articles/plurals Chinese omits; check every countable noun.
6. **Terminology** — `glossary_zh_en.md` is authoritative; define abbreviations at first use.
7. **Numbers & units** — keep values exactly; SI units with a space ("50 nm/s", "1 A"); ranges as in Ren's papers.
8. **No additions** — do not add explanations, claims or hedges absent from the source; mark unclear source text `[TODO: ...]`.
9. List any term you were unsure about in the report.
